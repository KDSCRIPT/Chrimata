# 🧠 AI Cost Optimizer

**AI Cost Optimizer** is an intelligent platform designed to plan, optimize, and analyze the costs of Generative AI (GenAI) workflows. It enables organizations to accelerate AI adoption by automatically generating architectures, recommending the best-fit tools, providing transparent cost breakdowns, and projecting ROI—fully tailored to your specific use case.

---

## 🚩 The Problem

Organizations struggle with GenAI adoption due to:

- ⚙️ **Overwhelming tool choices** and lack of clarity on where to start
- 💸 **Unpredictable and opaque costs** across setup, operations, and scaling
- 🕒 **Manual and time-consuming planning**, delaying deployment
- 📉 **Difficulty measuring ROI**, making it hard to justify investments

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**AI Cost Optimizer** provides an automated, end-to-end solution:

- ⚡ **AI-Powered Workflow Generator**  
  Auto-generates optimal GenAI architectures based on your requirements using OpenAI models.

- 🧩 **Smart Tool Recommendation Engine**  
  A FAISS-based RAG system suggests the most suitable tools for your needs with reasoning.

- 💰 **Transparent Cost Analysis**  
  Detailed setup, monthly, and yearly cost breakdowns—including optimization recommendations.

- 📊 **ROI Calculator**  
  Predictive ROI projections and timeline analysis to support confident investment decisions.

---

## 🖥️ How It Works

1. **Upload Inputs**  
   Provide your company profile, financials, and GenAI objectives.

2. **AI-Powered Analysis**  
   Our AI agents analyze your data using domain knowledge and best practices.

3. **Tool & Cost Recommendations**  
   FAISS RAG engine recommends tools and presents a transparent cost analysis.

4. **Report Generation**  
   Receive a downloadable report detailing your GenAI architecture, projected costs, ROI, and timeline.
